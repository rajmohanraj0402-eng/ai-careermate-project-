Problem Statement 01 — AI CareerMate
Smart Career Guidance, Internship & Skill Roadmap System
Problem Statement
Students often find it difficult to identify suitable career roles, understand industry-required skills, discover relevant internship opportunities, and create a structured learning roadmap. The proposed system aims to develop an AI-powered career guidance platform that analyzes a student's profile, interests, skills, projects, and career goals to provide personalized career and internship guidance.
Requirements
Student registration/login
Student profile and resume upload
Education, skills, projects and certifications
Career interest and target-role selection
Job/internship information
Job Description (JD) input or collection
AI-based career recommendation
Skill-gap analysis
Internship/job matching
Personalized learning roadmap
AI Analysis
Analyze student skills and interests
Identify suitable career roles
Compare student skills with job/internship requirements
Identify missing skills
Recommend technologies and learning resources
Generate a personalized career roadmap
Expected Output
Recommended career roles
Suitable internship/job opportunities
Job/role match score
Existing and missing skills
Skill-gap report
Personalized learning roadmap
Recommended projects and certifications
